# DIVE DEEP: storyboard

**Concept:** a DM from Ryan Ang that sets itself in type. Every word you hear lands on screen the moment it's spoken, and the O inside each problem word turns into the instrument that measures it: a lead-source chart, an automation meter, a clock. Then the camera dives into the process and comes out at a free consultation. The last frame is the first frame, an empty DM box with a caret, so the ad loops.

The film keeps the first cut's vocabulary: one typeface, a forward-only camera, and the client's own workflow shapes (applied, screened, booked, done). Nothing cuts and nothing fades. Each scene is built from the previous one's shapes and type: lines roll up and out of their band like a slot reel, and the shared O travels from word to word. On every beat the camera creeps a step closer to whichever big word is on screen; the big words sit on a nearer plane than the small lines, so only they grow.

The sound tells the same story. The problem half has drums, a pad and a ticking clock but no tune. The melody arrives with the offer.

120 BPM · beat = 0.5 s · 22 s = 11 bars. Bar lines fall half a beat after the loop seam, so RECRUITMENT, LEADS, TIME, FREE and DM each land on a downbeat. Times below are for `am_michael`; `af_heart` lands within 0.16 s of them, and the picture follows whichever voice is chosen.
